What the analysis looks for

The ingredient behind the meal

It looks up what's actually in the products you eat, so if two different foods upset you and they share one ingredient, it finds that ingredient.

The times you felt fine

If you ate something five times and only felt bad twice, it tries to work out why. A bigger portion, a bad night's sleep, something else on the plate. If it can't explain it, it tells you it isn't sure.

Your drinks and cigarettes

Coffee, alcohol and smoking all change how your gut behaves. Log them with one tap and they stop getting blamed on whatever you had for dinner.

What to cut next

You get a list of foods that are fine, foods that look like a problem, and what to leave out for a week to be sure. It will never tell you to eat something to test it.
